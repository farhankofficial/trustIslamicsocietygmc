Our Background

A conscious effort to understand, reconnect & grow.

Founded in 2024, Trust Islamic Society, Gomal Medical College emerged from a collective desire to address challenges students may face in a contemporary academic environment.

Why We Began

Among the challenges students encounter are emotional detachment from Deen-e-Islam, limited intellectual engagement with Islamic beliefs and principles, and cultural norms being perceived or presented as part of Islam.

Students may also encounter contemporary narratives and slogans that create confusion or doubts and can obscure a sound and comprehensive understanding of Islam.

Our response is a supportive platform where students can reconnect with their Deen, seek authentic knowledge, and engage with contemporary questions through the framework of the Qur’an and Sunnah.

Three Connected Dimensions

01

Emotional Detachment from Deen

A gradual weakening of a student's spiritual connection with Islam and its guidance.

02

Lack of Intellectual Understanding

Insufficient engagement with the intellectual foundations, worldview, and principles of Islam.

03

Confusing Culture with Deen

The conflation of cultural practices and social norms with authentic Islamic teachings.

Together, these influences can obscure the beauty, depth, and relevance of Islam during a formative stage of intellectual, personal, and professional development.

Our Mission

Uniting students to learn, live & grow.

Trust Islamic Society GMC aims to provide a platform for students to unite, grow, and thrive in a supportive community, to equip them with knowledge of Quran and Sunnah, and to encourage them to put this knowledge into practice so that they may align their academic pursuits and all other aspects of life with Islamic teachings.
