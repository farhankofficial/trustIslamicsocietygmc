Our Background

A conscious effort to understand, reconnect & grow.

Founded in 2024, Trust Islamic Society, Gomal Medical College emerged from a collective desire to address challenges students may face in a contemporary academic environment.

Why We Began

Students may experience emotional detachment from Deen-e-Islam, limited intellectual engagement with Islamic beliefs and principles, and cultural norms being presented as part of Islam.

Our response is a supportive platform where students can reconnect with their Deen, seek authentic knowledge, and engage contemporary questions through the framework of the Qur’an and Sunnah.

Three Connected Dimensions

01

Emotional Detachment from Deen

A weakening of a student's spiritual connection with Islam and its guidance.

02

Lack of Intellectual Understanding

Insufficient engagement with the intellectual foundations and worldview of Islam.

03

Confusing Culture with Deen

The conflation of cultural practices and social norms with authentic Islamic teachings.

Together, these influences can obscure the beauty, depth, and relevance of Islam during a formative stage of intellectual, personal, and professional development.

Our Mission

Uniting students to learn, live & grow.

Trust Islamic Society GMC aims to provide a platform for students to unite, grow, and thrive in a supportive community, equip them with knowledge of Quran and Sunnah, and encourage them to put this knowledge into practice in academic and personal life.
